Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

AI Video Studio is a personal application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

The app requests a single Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in channel. It does not read, list, edit or delete any other YouTube or Google account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth credentials Google issues are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by this app, and no data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Google API Services

AI Video Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads use the YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
